Hiroshima & Miyajima in one day

Two places, one day, no wasted hours. The Peace Memorial Museum in the morning, then the ferry to Miyajima for Itsukushima Shrine and the Great Torii. This is the free sample guide - the full version is free to read in full.

Can you really do both in one day?

Yes - if you start at the museum when it opens and take the direct train-and-ferry route to Miyajima. Hiroshima Station to Miyajimaguchi takes about 25-30 minutes on the JR Sanyo Line (about ¥420), and the JR ferry crossing is 10 minutes. The mistake to avoid is doing Miyajima first: the museum is at its calmest, and most meaningful, in its first hour.

The day costs about ¥1,840 in transport and admissions without a rail pass - and about ¥600 with a JR Pass, because the pass covers the train and the ferry fare. Note: the ¥100 Miyajima visitor tax is not covered by the JR Pass; everyone pays it.

The one thing to check before you go: the tide

The Great Torii changes completely with the tide. At high tide - a tide level of roughly 250 cm or more - it floats on the sea, the classic postcard view. At low tide, around 100 cm or less, you can walk out across the sand and stand at its base. Neither is better, but they are different days out, and the ferry even runs special sailings that pass by the torii between 9:10 and 16:10. Check the tide table for Miyajima before you fix your date.

Good to know

  • The Peace Memorial Museum asks for an online reservation for the first and last hour of the day; hours change with the season.
  • The Great Torii's long repair finished in December 2022 - it is fully visible, with no scaffolding.
  • For okonomiyaki in the city, Okonomimura gathers many small shops under one roof; each shop keeps its own hours.
