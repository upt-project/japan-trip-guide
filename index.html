Eat the dish the city is famous for

Every city has a bowl with its name on it - Hakata ramen, Osaka kushikatsu, Hiroshima okonomiyaki. The guides name a specific shop, the right hour, and the price before you sit down.

How ordering food works

Nights are eaten standing up

Yatai food stalls, market arcades, izakaya counters - Japan's evenings run on small places, small dishes and cash. The one thing to learn first is what lands on the bill.

The journey is part of the sightseeing

A torii gate standing in the sea at high tide, Mt Fuji sliding past a train window - in Japan the view between the stops is often the one you remember. The guides time the day around it.
