Osaka in one day

Six stops, one day, no wasted hours: the castle in the morning, the market and Dotonbori at midday, Tsutenkaku in the afternoon, and the Umeda Sky Building for sunset. The full guide is published on Substack on 7 December - the printable PDF is available now.

The day at a glance

WhenWhatCost
8:00Start at Umeda / JR Osaka Station - bags into a coin locker or Crosta OsakaLockers about ¥400-¥700

AfternoonWalk Dotonbori and Shinsaibashi, then Shinsekai and Tsutenkaku (timed reservation)¥1,500
SunsetUmeda Sky Building - sunset and the night view, then dinner in Umeda¥2,000

The day costs ¥5,610 pay-per-ride without lunch, ¥7,260 with it - including ¥910 of Osaka Metro fares. A Japan Rail Pass helps by exactly ¥0 inside Osaka: every ride on this route is Metro. The Osaka Amazing Pass (¥3,500) barely pays on this route, and its free Umeda Sky entry ends at 15:00 - before the sunset you came for.

Left for day two, on purpose

Universal Studios Japan is a full day of its own - adding it to a sightseeing day is the most common Osaka mistake. The bay area (Kaiyukan aquarium) and Abeno Harukas also belong to a second day. The full guide explains the swaps.

Good to know

  • Tsutenkaku needs a timed reservation - book the slot before the day, not at the tower.
  • Kuromon Market has no market-wide opening hours; each shop sets its own. The information centre runs 9:00-18:00.
  • Crosta Osaka, the staffed luggage counter at JR Osaka Station, is ¥1,000 per item per day, card only, 9:00-20:00 - at the 8:00 start, use a coin locker instead.
