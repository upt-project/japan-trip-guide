Kyoto in one day

Kyoto in one day is not a distance problem - it is a choosing problem. This route picks four stops, lines them up in one arc across the city, and leaves Arashiyama for a second day on purpose.

The route: one arc, no backtracking

TimeStop
7:00-8:40Fushimi Inari Taisha - arrive early, walk the torii gates before the crowds (free, open 24 hours)
9:30-11:00Kiyomizu-dera, then the preserved lanes of Sannenzaka down towards Gion
12:30-13:15Lunch in Gion
14:30-15:30Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion (¥500, open 9:00-17:00)
16:30-17:30Back in central Kyoto on the 205 bus

The whole day costs ¥1,850 before lunch - ¥850 of transport and ¥1,000 of admissions. With lunch, budget roughly ¥2,930-¥3,720. The Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass (¥1,100) does not pay off on this route, and a JR Pass only helps with ¥1,700 of it - pay per ride with an IC card.

Why Arashiyama is not in this guide

The bamboo grove sits on the far side of the city and honestly needs half a day of its own. Squeezing it into the same day as Fushimi Inari and Kinkaku-ji turns all three into a rush. The full guide explains the trade-off and what to swap if Arashiyama matters more to you than Kinkaku-ji.

Good to know

  • Fushimi Inari is free and open around the clock - the early start is about the crowds, not the gate times.
  • Kiyomizu-dera opens at 6:00 and admission is ¥500.
  • In Gion, photography is banned on the private lanes - signs mark them, and fines apply.
